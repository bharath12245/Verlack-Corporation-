SECTION 04
Complete Service Ecosystem
Verlak Corporation's service architecture is designed as an integrated ecosystem — not a menu of products.
Each service connects to the others, creating a holistic wealth relationship that deepens over the client lifecycle.
This architecture mirrors the full capabilities of UBS, Goldman Sachs, and J.P. Morgan Private Bank,
contextualised for India's regulatory and cultural framework.
01 WEALTH MANAGEMENT
The cornerstone Verlak service. A comprehensive, goals-based wealth management mandate covering the full
spectrum of a client's financial life.
■ Comprehensive financial planning & goal mapping
■ Asset allocation strategy across equity, debt, gold, real estate, alternatives
■ Regular portfolio rebalancing and performance attribution
■ Tax-efficient investment structuring
■ Insurance gap analysis and recommendations
■ Annual wealth health review
■ Intergenerational wealth planning integration
02 PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT (PMS)
SEBI-registered discretionary and non-discretionary portfolio management for HNI/UHNI clients seeking direct
equity ownership with institutional-grade management.
■ Verlak Conviction Portfolio — concentrated high-conviction Indian equities
■ Verlak Quality Compounder — QGLP-inspired long-term wealth creation
■ Verlak Balanced Advantage — dynamic equity-debt allocation
■ Verlak Special Situations — event-driven and deep-value strategies
■ Direct equity ownership (not fund wrappers) for tax efficiency
■ Monthly performance reporting with attribution analysis
■ Minimum ticket: ■50 lakhs (SEBI PMS threshold)
03 INVESTMENT ADVISORY
Research-driven, conflict-free investment advisory for clients who prefer to self-execute with expert guidance —
the purest fiduciary model.
■ Personalized investment policy statement (IPS) development
■ Mutual fund portfolio construction and optimization
■ ETF and index strategy for cost-efficient core allocation
■ Fixed income and bond ladder strategies
■ IPO and primary market advisory
■ Regular market outlook and tactical allocation views
■ Fee-only model: zero commission, complete transparency
